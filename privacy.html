Privacy Policy — PhotoScan: Print Scanner

Effective date: November 16, 2026

PhotoScan: Print Scanner ("PhotoScan") digitizes printed photos on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. PhotoScan does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

Your photos

All processing — capturing a printed photo, adjusting its corners, correcting perspective, and saving the result — happens entirely on your device. Corrected photos are saved to the Photos library on your iPhone only. No photo or image data ever leaves your device through the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Lifetime Unlock" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. The app only checks, on your device, whether the unlock has been purchased.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
